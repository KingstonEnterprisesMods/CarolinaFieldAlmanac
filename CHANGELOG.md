# Changelog - Carolina Field Almanac

## 1.0.6
- Public beta.
- Description, README and in-game texts corrected: Carolina Field Almanac is NOT compatible with other weather or hay drying mods (the old text was wrong). Canada and Europe stations and European insurance are now listed. Old lines removed (4 % per wet day, hay curing days). Credit line for Canadian weather data added (Open Government Licence - Canada). Nothing changes in how the mod plays.

## 1.0.5
- New NOAA station Monroe 26 N (LA). Jones La and La-Ark Logging now pre-pick it; AR River Lowlands pre-picks Pine Bluff; BlackRiver Farms pre-picks Alexandria (towns confirmed by the Delta South Modding & Mapping team).

## 1.0.4
- 7 new NOAA weather stations near Delta South maps: Greenwood and Greenville (MS), Alexandria (LA), Jonesboro and Blytheville (AR), Cape Girardeau (MO) and Troy (AL). Nine Delta South maps now pre-pick their nearest station.
- Map crops that are a type of corn, soybean or sorghum (non-GMO, eco, bio, food-grade, cattle-feed, early, new, organic, feed) now use the plain crop's weather numbers and crop insurance (cattle-feed beans are not insured, same as feed beans).

## 1.0.3
- The Field Cure box now shows only when you are on or near the cut hay (within 50 m), not anywhere on a large farmland. Missed notices and the rain-on-hay line still show everywhere.

## 1.0.2
- The Field Cure panel in a vehicle now sits bottom-left, right above the mini map, next to the Field Almanac map icons.

## 1.0.1
- Hay curing follows the game calendar (days per month), like the crop clocks: each game day stands for the real days of that month. Drying, dew, rain and respiration all scale the same way. Cut at the right time.

## 1.0.0
- ModHub-ready cleanup (GIANTS TestRunner 15/15). Nothing changes in how the mod plays.
