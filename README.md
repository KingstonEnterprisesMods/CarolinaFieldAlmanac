# Carolina Field Almanac (FS25) — Public Beta 1.0.6

**By Kingston Enterprises** · Farming Simulator 25 · Single player and multiplayer (multiplayer is beta)

Carolina Field Almanac puts **real weather and real crop research** into your farm.
Every number in the mod comes from a named source. If no sourced number exists for something, the mod has no effect on it.
The **Sources** tab in the game lists every number and where it came from.

> **This is a public beta (test build).** It is played and tested every day, but you may still hit bugs. Please report them (see below).

## Features

- **Weather from real climate records**
  - 426 NOAA stations in all 50 U.S. states and 84 in Canada's farm provinces.
  - 815 in Europe: Poland, Germany, the UK, Ireland and France.
  - Each day's high, low, rain and sun come from 30 years of records (1991-2020).
  - Rain timing, thunder, snow, humidity, wind and clouds follow hourly records for your station.
  - The game's own forecast and sky follow these days.
- **Crops with their own clock:** every field keeps its own growth clock (degree days) from planting, fed by the real weather.
- **Sourced crop damage:** freeze, heat, hail, wind, tornado, hurricane, drought, waterlogging, flooding, rain on a ready crop and harvest delay. You can switch each type on or off.
- **Crop insurance, the real way:**
  - U.S.: buy a policy per crop before you plant (Yield Protection, Revenue Protection, RP-HPE or APH, 50-85 %), using USDA RMA numbers.
  - Poland, Germany and France use their own countries' numbers. UK and Ireland: crop insurance is not in the mod yet. It stays off until we find sourced UK and Irish numbers.
- **Field cure hay:** cut it, leave it lie, and let the sun cure it in the field. Grass dries from sun, heat and dry air (USDA-ARS drying research), takes back a little dew at night, and gets soaked by rain.
- **Map pre-picks:** 81 maps that name a real place pick their nearest station for you (you confirm it).

## What you need

- **Nothing else.** Carolina Field Almanac works on its own (no other mods needed).

**Not compatible:** do NOT use it with another weather or hay drying mod: Realistic Water & Soil Management, Moisture System, Improved Hay, Realistic Hay Drying, or Seasons-type mods that change the weather or calendar. With Realistic Water & Soil Management or Moisture System installed, the Almanac's weather, crop weather damage, insurance and Field Cure hay curing are all off. Use only one weather mod.

## Install

1. Download `FS25_CarolinaFieldAlmanac.zip` from the [Releases](../../releases) page.
2. Put the zip **as is** (do not unzip it) in your mods folder: `Documents\My Games\FarmingSimulator2025\mods`.
3. Start the game and tick **Carolina Field Almanac** in the mod list.
4. On a new save, a window asks you to pick your weather station. Change it any time in **ESC -> Settings -> Field Almanac**, or on the Field Almanac page's **Settings** tab.

## Report a bug

1. Quit the game normally (main menu -> Quit).
2. **Before you start the game again** (the game wipes the log on every start), copy this file: `Documents\My Games\FarmingSimulator2025\log.txt`
3. Open an [Issue](../../issues) with:
   - the log,
   - your map,
   - your station,
   - a screenshot if you have one,
   - a few words on what happened.

## Data sources

U.S.: NOAA (GHCN-Daily, U.S. Climate Normals 1991-2020, hourly normals), USDA (RMA, NASS), U.S. Extension services and peer-reviewed papers, each named on the Sources tab.

Canada: Environment and Climate Change Canada data — contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - Canada.

Europe:
- **Poland:** IMGW-PIB; Ministry of Agriculture (MRiRW).
- **Germany:** Deutscher Wetterdienst; GDV; Vereinigte Hagel.
- **UK:** Met Office MIDAS Open (contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0).
- **Ireland:** Met Eireann (CC BY 4.0).
- **France:** Meteo-France (Licence Ouverte v2.0); CCR; FranceAgriMer.
